### Method pivot to a label-free support head; H100 plan written; paper restructured (no reported number changed)

- Literature check (arXiv API, 2026-11-01): three papers added to the related work. Thirumani Srinivasan and Nordstrom, "Does VGGT Know What It Does Not Know?" (2610.35806, 26 Oct 2026): a linear probe on frozen VGGT features predicts camera-pose failure with AUROC 0.935 against 0.797 for native confidence; pose failure, not unobserved geometry, but the closest recipe to ours and now a baseline (`vggt_oialin`). Hillemann et al., "Uncertainty Quality of VGGT" (2606.16479): native uncertainty vs reconstruction error on DTU. Li et al., "When Wider Views Fail" (2609.24839): wide viewpoint spans produce unsupported geometry, no per-point labels. No paper found that labels unobserved geometry per point or detects it from features.
- Decision: the 100M discrete diffusion head and continual adaptation are moved to future work. In their place the paper proposes the **support head** (`scripts/oia_head.py`): a few-million-parameter conv head on the frozen multi-view features (VGGT: aggregator tokens of the four DPT layers; Pi3X: the 2048-d tokens feeding its confidence head) that predicts P(imagined) per pixel. Training is label-free: the benchmark's sphere masking is applied to the backbone's *own* clean prediction (predicted depth and cameras place and project the sphere), fills gray/blur/noise, 15% clean samples with an all-observed target, 79 DTU training scans, inpaint held out, CO3Dv2 never seen. Variants: `_oiafuse` (fusion with native), `_oialin` (linear probe), `_oiapatch` (per-image encoder features only: a patch detector, the main control), `_oiagt` (GT-placed masks). Model names route through `hallu3d.make_model`.

The real feature hooks (`vggt_forward_feats`, `pi3_forward_feats`) are written against the public model code and are untested until the GPU; `run_h100.py check` prints their shapes first.

### P2-stress launched (2026-10-28, kernel popsensharma/hallu3d-p2-stress)
