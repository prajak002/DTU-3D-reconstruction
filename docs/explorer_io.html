HalluBench-3D · Observe, Imagine, Abstain · every number below comes from a logged run

When a 3D model has not seen a surface, does it say so?

Pick an input case: a dataset, a scene, a model, how the hidden region is filled, how large it is, and which placement. The page shows what the model returned, how much of the invented geometry passed its own confidence filter, and how the other models did on the identical input.

INPUT

Ten photos with one 3D region hidden

A sphere is placed on the ground-truth surface; every pixel in every view whose true 3D point lies inside it is hidden and filled (gray, blur, noise or an inpainting). The model never sees that surface.

OUTPUT

Depth and confidence at every pixel

Pixels inside the sphere are unsupported (U): no camera saw them. Pixels well outside are supported (S). We score the model's own confidence on U against S.

READING

CUR is the number to watch

Keep the confidence threshold that retains 90% of supported pixels. CUR is the share of unsupported pixels that also pass. 0 is ideal; 0.9 is a blind guesser. AUROC 1 means confidence separates the two perfectly; 0.5 means it carries no information.
