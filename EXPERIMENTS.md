## How to run on the AWS L4 box

The box (`g6.xlarge`, eu-central-1, one L4) has the repository at `~/hallu`, the DTU training set at `~/data/dtu` (Cameras, Depths and lights 1, 3, 5 of every scan; the zip was deleted to make room) and the preprocessed CO3Dv2 subset at `~/data/co3d`. `source ~/env.sh` sets PATH (`/opt/pytorch` venv, torch 2.12 cu130), PYTHONPATH (Pi3 clone + `~/hallu/scripts`) and the HF token. ## Gates

- **Gate 3 (P4-vis, pre-registered 2026-11-06 before any vis head was trained).** The visibility-trained head `<base>_oiavis` counts as detecting missing support when, on the DTU vis protocol pooled over 3 / 5 / 10 views, (a) it beats native confidence on AUROC and CUR (paired sign-flip p < 0.05) and (b) it beats its per-image control `<base>_oiavispatch` on AUROC by **at least 0.02** with p < 0.05 (the Gate 2 clause "any positive difference at p < 0.05" was too weak and is not reused), and (c) the same two conditions hold on at least one dataset the head never saw (CO3Dv2 or ETH3D). The fill-trained head `<base>_oia` is reported on the same rows as the transfer test of patch detection. If (b) fails, the paper reports that the single-view-support signal is also recoverable from per-image features (image borders, depth discontinuities) and the method claim is dropped for good.

- **Gate 4 (P5-visself, pre-registered 2026-11-06 16:51 UTC, before any label-free head was trained; kernels `hallu3d-p5-vggt` and `hallu3d-p5-pi3x` launched at that time from commit c7e6919).** The label-free visibility head `<base>_oiavisself` is trained on the same clean-image protocol as `<base>_oiavis` but its labels come from `hallu3d.support_count` applied to the backbone's OWN predicted depth and cameras (the 20% least confident pixels dropped, as for the fill-trained self labels); no ground truth is used at any point. It counts as the method of record when, on the DTU vis protocol pooled over 3 / 5 / 10 views AND on CO3Dv2 pooled over 3 / 5 views, (a) it beats native confidence on AUROC and CUR (paired sign-flip p < 0.05), (b) it beats its per-image control `<base>_oiavisselfpatch` on AUROC by at least 0.02 at p < 0.05, and (c) it is within 0.03 AUROC of the ground-truth-trained head `<base>_oiavis` on DTU and beats reprojection consistency `<base>_reproj` on AUROC at p < 0.05 on CO3Dv2 (the training signal is the reprojection test itself, so the head has to generalise better than its teacher). If (a) or (b) fails, the label-free head is dropped. If only (c) fails, it is reported as an ablation and the ground-truth-trained head stays the method. The per-layer probes (`vggt_oiavislin4/11/17/23`, `<base>_oiavispatchlin`) and the extra seeds (`<base>_oiavis_s1`, `_s2`) are descriptive: no gate, reported as measured (where visibility becomes linearly decodable; seed spread of the head).

- **Gate 5 (P10-prov, pre-registered 2026-11-07 18:31 UTC, before any provenance head was trained; the fourth H200 driver `h200_p10.sh` was queued at 18:28 UTC behind the third pass and starts training only after it).** The label-free provenance head `<base>_oiaprovself` outputs, for every pixel of every view, one probability per other input view that this view corroborates the pixel; its support score is the probability that no view does. It counts as the method of record (replacing the conv head `<base>_oiavisself` in the main table) when, on the vis protocol pooled as in Table vis: (a) its support score is within 0.02 AUROC of `<base>_oiavisself` on every dataset for every backbone (the structured output costs nothing), (b) its attribution beats the reprojection test with the model's own cameras (`<base>_reproj`, per-pair agreement) on prov-AUROC at paired sign-flip p < 0.05 on DTU and on at least two of CO3Dv2, ETH3D and the landmark photographs, for at least three of the four backbones, and (c) its top-1 view is a seeing view for at least 90% of the supported pixels on DTU. If (a) fails, the provenance head is reported as an attribution add-on and the conv head stays the support score. If (b) fails, the attribution is reported as measured and the architecture claim is reduced to reading per-view evidence at one forward, at the cost of reprojection or below. If (c) fails, the count and the ranking are reported but "which view" is not claimed. The ground-truth-trained `<base>_oiaprov` is the upper bound and is descriptive. The pass conditions are not changed after this line.

- **Gate 1 (Nov 1):** on P1-full, CUR mean with 95% CI excluding 0 and AUROC clearly below 1 for both models. Fail: models already abstain; report that, pivot to method-only or stop.
- **Gate 2 (Nov 7):** on P3-main, disagreement beats native confidence on AUROC and CUR, paired sign-flip p < 0.05 over scans, at equal compute. Fail: paper = benchmark + best training-free detector.
